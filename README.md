## Why Withink

Most writing apps are built to keep you scrolling. Withink is built to get you
off the screen and back into your day.

- **One entry per day** — your diary is a calm sequence of days, not an infinite feed of posts.
- **Private by default** — your pages belong to you alone. Not to an algorithm, not to an audience.
- **Built for years, not weeks** — flashbacks, insights, and streaks are designed around long-term reflection, so your diary gets more valuable every year you keep it.

## What's inside

### ✍️ Write

- A **distraction-free editor** that fills the whole screen — rich text, quotes, lists, images, nothing else asking for your attention.
- **Mood tracking** on every entry, drawn in warm ink rather than cold charts.
- **Autosave with visible state**, so you always know your words are safe.
- **Works offline** — write on the train; Withink syncs when you're back.

### 🕰️ Remember

- **Flashbacks** resurface entries from your past, exactly when they matter.
- **Insights** turn months of writing into a quiet heatmap, word volume, and mood trends.
- **Streaks** encourage gently — never guilt-trip.

### 🔒 Keep it yours

- **Optional zero-knowledge encryption** — lock your diary with a password that never leaves your device, so even we couldn't read your entries.
- **Diary Lock** — a PIN gate with auto-lock for when you share a device.
- **Export everything** anytime — entries and photos, as a ZIP you own.
- **Delete means delete** — closing your account removes your entries, photos, and data for real.

### 📱 Everywhere

- Designed **phone-first**: a native-feeling tab bar, thumb-sized controls, offline support.
- Also at home on desktop, with a classic two-column reading layout.
- Warm paper theme for the day, low-strain dark desk for the night.

## Questions

**Who can read my entries?**
Only you. Entries are stored privately, and if you enable zero-knowledge encryption, they're encrypted on your device with a key we never see — even we couldn't read them.

**Is the free plan actually usable?**
Yes — write today's entry every single day, search, reflect, export. Paid plans add a longer editing window for older days, more photo storage, and more simultaneous devices.

**Can I take my diary with me?**
Always. Export your full diary — entries and images — as a ZIP whenever you want. No lock-in, no permission needed.

**What happens if I stop paying?**
Your diary stays yours. You drop to the Free plan's limits; nothing is deleted.

**Does it work offline?**
Yes. Write without a connection and Withink saves locally, then syncs safely once you're back online.

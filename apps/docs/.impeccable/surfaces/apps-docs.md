# Surface Brief: withink.me Landing Page (apps/docs)

- **Scope:** the public marketing home page at `/` in `apps/docs` (withink.me). Nav, hero, method, live demo grid, privacy, CTA, footer.
- **Mode:** Persuade — the visitor decides and acts (sign up today). Design is the product here.
- **Audience:** a first-time visitor who journals or wants to start. They should believe, within seconds: withink. is a calm, private, encrypted daily journal that keeps their ordinary days — then sign up.
- **Job / action:** primary action is signing up / "Open Your Diary" (→ `${APP_URL}/register`). Secondary: read the privacy philosophy. Nav = wordmark + theme toggle + one short primary button — "Sign In" when signed out, "Open" when signed in.
- **Proof / content:** interactive in-page demos of the real product (live editor + mood selector, Sanctuary Lock PIN pad, flashback + reflection note, memory-page keepsake lightbox, compact writing calendar + mood distribution, ZIP export). Keep all live demos. Mood icons are lucide (Angry/Frown/Meh/Smile/SmilePlus), matching the real app — never emoji.
- **Constraints:** world is The Field Ledger, kept as a diary (DESIGN.md): manila/umber surfaces, iron-gall sepia ink / parchment text, single warm old-paper gold accent `#C39553` (`oklch(0.70 0.10 75)`), selection and rings in that gold; rounded corners matching the app (`0.75rem`), hairline borders over soft shadows; exactly two typefaces — Alegreya serif for all printed text, Caveat hand for field notes (no mono); hero is a plate-free statement of words on the desk; 100–250ms motion; dual light/dark theme with the toggle. Diary vocabulary — never cabinet/collection/accession. No invented claims. Pricing undecided — do NOT claim a price ("No credit card. No trackers. No upsells. Free to start.").
- **Chosen direction (current):** The Field Ledger (concept-seed ea00c61c), refined by user feedback across rounds: removed the engraved leaf, the hero plate card, and the "cabinet" framing; switched vocabulary to writer/diary (hero CTA "Open Your Diary", PIN demo = real "Sanctuary Lock", mood prompt "How are you feeling today?", "Saving…/Saved", "Export anytime"); rounded to the app's corners; shrank the heatmap to fixed 40px cells; dropped to two typefaces; moved the accent from moss-green to the warm old-paper gold `#C39553`; nav = wordmark + theme toggle + short "Sign In"/"Open" button; new hero copy — H1 "Your ordinary days are worth keeping.", hand note "the small days become the long story.", sub "A private, encrypted journal — one page a day, saved offline and exported anytime. Yours forever." Recorded in the layout's contract comment and DESIGN.md.
- **Memorable moment:** the words-on-the-desk hero (serif promise with an italic accent, hand-written note) and the living demos acting as proof on the scroll.
- **Undecided:** pricing wording deliberately omitted (undecided in PRODUCT.md); OG/social image asset still references `/og-image.png` (not yet authored for this world).

# Surface Brief: withink.me Landing Page (apps/docs)

- **Scope:** the public marketing home page at `/` in `apps/docs` (withink.me). Nav, hero, method, live demo grid, privacy, CTA, footer — plus the policy/reading pages (`/about`, `/contact`, `/privacy`, `/terms`) and the shared docs chrome they rely on.
- **Mode:** Persuade — the visitor decides and acts (sign up today). Design is the product here.
- **Audience:** a first-time visitor who journals or wants to start — now explicitly **phone-first** (most arrive from social links on mobile; baseline 375×812, stress width 320). They should believe, within seconds: withink. is a calm, private, encrypted daily journal that keeps their ordinary days — then sign up.
- **Job / action:** primary action is signing up / "Open Your Diary" (→ `${APP_URL}/register`). Secondary: read the privacy philosophy. Nav = wordmark + theme toggle + one short primary button — "Sign In" when signed out, "Open" when signed in.
- **Proof / content:** interactive in-page demos of the real product (live editor + mood selector, Diary Lock PIN pad, flashback + reflection note, memory-page keepsake lightbox, compact writing calendar + mood distribution, ZIP export). Keep all live demos. Mood icons are lucide (Angry/Frown/Meh/Smile/SmilePlus), matching the real app — never emoji.
- **Constraints:** world is The Field Ledger, kept as a diary (DESIGN.md): manila/umber surfaces, iron-gall sepia ink / parchment text, single warm old-paper gold accent `#C39553` (`oklch(0.70 0.10 75)`), selection and rings in that gold; rounded corners matching the app (`0.75rem`), hairline borders over soft shadows; exactly two typefaces — Alegreya serif for all printed text, Caveat hand for field notes (no mono); hero is a plate-free statement of words on the desk; 100–250ms motion; dual light/dark theme with the toggle. Diary vocabulary — never cabinet/collection/accession. No invented claims. Pricing undecided — do NOT claim a price ("No credit card. No trackers. No upsells. Free to start.").
- **Chosen direction (current):** The Field Ledger (concept-seed ea00c61c), refined by user feedback across rounds: removed the engraved leaf, the hero plate card, and the "cabinet" framing; switched vocabulary to writer/diary (hero CTA "Open Your Diary", PIN demo = real "Diary Lock", mood prompt "How are you feeling today?", "Saving…/Saved", "Export anytime"); rounded to the app's corners; dropped to two typefaces; moved the accent to the warm old-paper gold `#C39553`; nav = wordmark + theme toggle + short "Sign In"/"Open" button; hero copy — H1 "Your ordinary days are worth keeping.", hand note "the small days become the long story.", sub "A private, encrypted journal — one page a day, saved offline and exported anytime. Yours forever." Recorded in the layout's contract comment and DESIGN.md.
- **Memorable moment:** the words-on-the-desk hero (serif promise with an italic accent, hand-written note) and the living demos acting as proof on the scroll — now fully operable by thumb.
- **Phone-first pass (Phase 5 decisions):**
  - Demo plates are full-width stacked cards on phones; desktop-tile min-heights (`min-h-95/80/75`) apply from `md:` up only.
  - Every demo works by pure touch: mood selector is `grid-cols-5` at every width (labels drop to 10px/untracked on phones so "RADIANT" fits at 320; 53px targets); PIN pad keys are 48px on phones, 40px from `sm:`; flashback note input and submit are 44px.
  - Polaroid keepsakes: hover-only tilt is gone as the affordance. Cards carry a gentle staggered idle sway (motion, disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`), tap opens the lightbox, and the caption honestly reads "Tap any keepsake to enlarge". Pointer hover still straightens/lifts as a non-essential enhancement.
  - Heatmap day details open by tap only (the group-hover tooltip is deleted); the tap path is the day-vignette dialog, so no information is hover-gated anywhere.
  - Both hand-rolled overlays are replaced by `@withink/ui/dialog` (centered card at every width — desktop presentation preserved): Radix owns focus trap, Escape, and scroll lock; the shared overlay motion system (200ms in / 150ms out) owns movement. The lightbox is index-based with drag-to-swipe between photos (64px + velocity threshold, same gesture contract as the app's media lightbox), an "n / N" counter chip, 44px prev/next IconButtons, ArrowLeft/Right keys, and `next/image` throughout. The vignette dialog keeps the July-N header, mood badge, quote, words count, and "kept for later." hand note.
  - Hero: headline uses the fluid `text-hero` / `md:text-display` utilities (no fixed step); CTAs stack full-width at 44px on phones; `initial={false}` keeps the headline LCP-visible before hydration.
  - Header: CTA is `h-11 md:h-10`, header pads `env(safe-area-inset-top)`; footer pads `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` and its links are 44px rows; docs root layout now exports `viewportFit: "cover"` + dual theme-color metas (#eee5d6 / #1e170f).
  - Reading pages (`/about`, `/contact`, `/privacy`, `/terms`) are top-anchored with bounded `py-10 md:py-16` (no vertical-centering assumptions); the contact form is single-column with 44px name/email/submit controls, correct autocomplete attributes, a 44px copy-email IconButton with visible copied feedback, and 44px social rows.
- **Undecided:** pricing wording deliberately omitted (undecided in PRODUCT.md); OG/social image asset still references `/og-image.png` (not yet authored for this world).
